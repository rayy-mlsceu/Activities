Sinigang na Baka

Sinigang na Baka is a classic Filipino soup made with tender beef, fresh vegetables, and a savory tamarind broth. It is a comforting meal that is best served hot with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs beef ribs or beef neck bones
  • 1 pack sinigang sa sampalok mix
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 pieces gabi (taro)
  • 1 radish, sliced
  • 10 pieces okra
  • 10 pieces sitaw (string beans)
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 1 bunch kangkong (water spinach)
  • Fish sauce to taste
  • 8 cups water

Instructions

  1. Bring the water to a boil and cook the beef until tender, removing any foam that rises to the top.
  2. Add the onion, tomatoes, gabi, and radish, then simmer until the vegetables begin to soften.
  3. Stir in the sinigang mix and season with fish sauce according to your taste.
  4. Add the eggplant, okra, and string beans, then cook for several minutes.
  5. Mix in the kangkong and cook for about one minute until wilted.
  6. Serve hot with freshly cooked white rice.
